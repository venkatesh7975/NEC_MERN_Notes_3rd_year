## Practice and exit evidence

Design notifications or inventory with replay, duplicate delivery, and operational limits.

Defend consistency and freshness requirements; demonstrate a failure matrix and measured scaling decision.

## Review and next step

Record a failed boundary or unclear explanation, repeat it with a changed input, and move to the next requirement only when you can demonstrate the behavior. Use [project status](../projects/README.md) to distinguish source from specifications.
