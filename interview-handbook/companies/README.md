## Build a company worksheet

Record company, role, level, job URL, confirmed rounds, time limits, allowed tools, and recruiter-confirmed preparation guidance. Identify the product's users and one workflow the role may touch. Map the job's requirements to concrete repository exercises. Leave unknowns marked unknown until confirmed.

For a frontend role, prioritize the browser and React bank plus timed UI builds. For a backend-heavy role, prioritize Node, MongoDB, security, concurrency, and API integration. For a general software role, allocate substantial time to DSA and language-independent reasoning; MERN alone is not sufficient.

## Behavioral story inventory

Prepare truthful stories for ownership, disagreement, learning, failure, ambiguity, collaboration, and a delivered improvement. Each should explain the situation, your responsibility, your action, and the actual outcome. Evidence can be a test, measured improvement, a delivered feature, or feedback you really received. Avoid invented commercial impact.

## Mock interview review

The reviewer records where clarification was missing, which boundary failed, what explanation was unclear, and which tradeoff lacked evidence. The candidate repeats the weak portion three days later with a changed input. A company logo or a large question count is not proof of readiness.
