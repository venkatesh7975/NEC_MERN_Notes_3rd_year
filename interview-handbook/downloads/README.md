[Open the native Google Sheets tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qv2QV0GggbD07a_JYlhir__9PxiIjKEQEbz103HR0H0/edit). Tabs, formulas, dates, validation, and question data were checked after conversion. Sharing follows the owner's Drive permissions. If you cannot access that copy, import the committed Excel file into your own Drive and open it with Google Sheets.

Editable inputs are pale amber. On Questions, enter a last-reviewed date; Next review uses the interval in Summary. Use 1, 3, 7, or 14 days as appropriate for your retrieval stage. Summary counts derive from the detail rows. Your progress starts blank.

CSV files have no formulas or validation. PDF and Word contain original repository material, not redistributed external course books. Explorer progress is independent of spreadsheet progress and stays in the current browser until exported.
